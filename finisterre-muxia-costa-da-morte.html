Finisterre, Muxía and Costa da Morte

DifficultyEasy Duration7-stop tour LocationSantiago de Compostela, Spain

Journey past the traditional end of the Camino de Santiago to the edge of the world. Explore the dramatic cliffs, crashing Atlantic breakers, and hidden fishing hamlets along Galicia's legendary Coast of Death.

Route highlights

  • Visit the iconic Cape Finisterre lighthouse and zero-kilometer stone
  • Explore the cliffside sanctuary of Muxía
  • Scenic stops at deep rias, cascading waterfalls, and local coastal villages
  • Expert local guiding with comfortable air-conditioned transport

Good to know before you book

  • Easy walking pace at each stop — suitable for most fitness levels
  • Coastal weather can be windy; a light waterproof jacket is worth packing
  • A good add-on if you're already walking the Camino and want to see Finisterre without a multi-day extension
