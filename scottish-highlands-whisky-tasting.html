Edinburgh, Scotland

Scottish Highlands tour and whisky tasting

DifficultyModerate DurationFull day LocationEdinburgh, Scotland

Venture north into the breathtaking Scottish Highlands on this curated small-group excursion. Traverse dramatic glens, behold mirror-like highland lochs, and experience authentic local heritage with an exclusive distillery tour and tasting session.

Route highlights

  • Small-group guided journey from central Edinburgh
  • Stop at historic Glencoe and scenic highland lookouts
  • Traditional single malt distillery tour and guided tasting
  • Comfortable coach transit with local expert commentary

Good to know before you book

  • Mostly coach-based with short walks — comfortable footwear is enough, no technical hiking gear needed
  • Tastings include alcohol; a soft-drink alternative is available on request
  • Runs rain or shine — bring a warm layer for outdoor stops
