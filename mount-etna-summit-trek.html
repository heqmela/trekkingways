Piano Provenzana, Sicily, Italy

Mount Etna summit trekking tour

DifficultyHard Duration5-6 hours LocationPiano Provenzana, Sicily, Italy

Conquer Europe's highest and most active volcano alongside a certified alpine volcanological guide. Trek across ancient lava flows, explore smoking summit craters, and take in sweeping panoramas across the Ionian coast.

Route highlights

  • Ascent with professional mountain and vulcanology guides
  • Traverse high-altitude volcanic ash fields and cinder cones
  • Up-close views of active summit fumaroles and craters
  • Safety equipment and briefing provided prior to departure

Good to know before you book

  • This is a genuine trek — good fitness, sturdy hiking boots, and layers are essential
  • Altitude and exposed terrain mean weather can shift quickly; check our Trail Prep Guide before you go
  • Not recommended for anyone with respiratory or mobility conditions
