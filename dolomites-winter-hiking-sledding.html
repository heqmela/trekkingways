Dolomites of Bolzano: winter hiking and sledding

DifficultyEasy to moderate DurationFull day SeasonWinter

The high plateaus above Bolzano are one of the easiest places in the Alps to walk in deep winter. Wide, groomed tracks cross open snowfields with the Dolomite peaks standing in the distance, so you get the scenery of a serious mountain day without needing any technical snow skill.

The walk ends the way South Tyroleans have always ended it: on a wooden toboggan, taking the run back down to the valley. Winter gear is provided, and the guide sets the pace to the group, which makes this a good first taste of the mountains in winter rather than a test of one.

Route highlights

  • A guided winter walk on wide, prepared snow tracks — no previous snow experience needed
  • Toboggan descent back to the valley to finish the day
  • Winter equipment provided by the operator
  • Dolomite peaks in view for most of the route

Good to know before you book

  • Runs in the winter season only, and depends on snow cover — check the dates on the listing
  • Waterproof boots with grip, warm layers and gloves are on you; the sledding gear is not
  • Short daylight in midwinter, so the day starts early
  • Cold-weather layering is covered in the Gear Checklist
